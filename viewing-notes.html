Using the online simulation

The presenter uses the AI-generated Steve Hogan voice. Original source films retain their original audio. Presenter narration has optional English captions. This recording cannot hear or respond to viewers.

Listen and reflect at a comfortable pace. Optional chat prompts can be answered privately or discussed with colleagues when viewing together. Pause playback for longer working time, then resume for the debrief.

  • The supplied programme consists of four live online sessions of 60 minutes each. Recording length is shorter because extended individual work, breakout periods and live contributions are replaced by brief eight-second pauses. Every structured activity is introduced and debriefed, with prompts to pause for longer.
  • Private notes are the default. Chat or a colleague discussion is offered only when a viewer is watching with a live group. No actual participant response, producer action, breakout room, whiteboard or chat message is claimed.
  • Participant Guide page references and the between-session thread are retained: students and routine review in Session 1; day map and Keep, Reduce, Explore in Session 2; brief, design, student check and commitment in Session 3; partnership, action plan, conversation plan and final reflection in Session 4.
  • A location-neutral Acknowledgement recognises the Countries viewers join from. A live presenter still needs to prepare confirmed local Country names and follow organisational protocol.
  • Detail-first processing, monotropism and capacity are presented as lenses for understanding individuals. Narration avoids assuming one brain profile, support need or inner experience applies to all Autistic students.
  • The simulation retains the guide content while avoiding clinical, diagnostic or detailed legal guidance. Current official DSE and NCCD information and local school processes remain the reference for procedural questions.
  • All original source documents remain unchanged. The supplied Secondary Online PDFs have 18, 18, 17 and 17 pages. All 70 PPTX slides are active and map one-to-one to those PDF pages.
  • Three embedded films are played once each in full. Duplicate media relationship references in the PPTX are deduplicated. Source hashes are verified. Narration introduces and reflects on each film without speaking over it.
